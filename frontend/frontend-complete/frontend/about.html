About DSE-Compass

Admission Support & College Comparison Portal for Diploma students seeking Direct Second Year Engineering (DSE) admissions in Maharashtra.

Problem

Students spend significant time visiting multiple websites, PDFs, and admission documents to compare colleges. The lack of a centralized platform makes decision-making difficult.

Our Solution

A responsive web application that allows students to search, filter, and compare engineering colleges using a centralized database — based on cutoffs, branches, location, placements, and college type.

Key Features

  • Search colleges by name or district
  • Filter by percentile range, branch, and college type
  • Side-by-side comparison of multiple colleges
  • View key statistics: cutoffs, packages, fees, branches
  • Mobile-friendly responsive interface

Target Users

  • Primary: Diploma students seeking DSE admissions, Engineering aspirants
  • Secondary: Parents, Academic counselors

Out of Scope

  • Admission form submission
  • Online counseling
  • Seat allotment prediction
  • User login / authentication
  • Payment gateway
